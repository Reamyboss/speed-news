NFF appoints Eguavoen as interim Super Falcons coach

Reported byDaily Trust· Seun Adeuyi·9 September 2026 at 10:03 WAT(2 hours ago)

From the source

The Nigeria Football Federation (NFF) has appointed Augustine Eguavoen as interim head coach of the Super Falcons ahead of the team’s 2028 Olympic Games qualifying campaign. Eguavoen, a former head coach of the Super Eagles, will take charge of the Falcons for their two-legged second-round Olympic qualifier against Comoros in October. He will be assisted by Christopher Danjuma, head coach of Nasarawa Amazons, while the technical team will also include Ayisat Yusuf, a former Super Falcons defender, and Olatunji Baruwa, a former Super Eagles goalkeeper trainer.
